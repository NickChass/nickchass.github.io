Data guide

What Counts as Progress? · 3 October 2026

The official archive contains seven CSV tables. The table roles and fields were checked against the original codebook and the acquired files. All dates are relative to the start of the corresponding module offering; day 0 is its start. The exports use ?, empty strings and NA as recognized missing-value tokens.

Table Actual rows Role
courses 22 Module-offering calendar
assessments 206 Assessment type, deadline and weight
studentRegistration 32,593 Registration and withdrawal
studentInfo 32,593 Enrollment context and final outcome
studentAssessment 173,912 Submitted work and recorded marks
vle 6,364 Learning-platform resource definitions
studentVle 10,655,280 Resource interaction records

Units and joining. A course offering is code_module + code_presentation; a learner enrollment adds id_student. An assessment joins by id_assessment, and a resource joins by module, presentation and id_site. All small-table keys and tested foreign keys pass. Learner identifiers repeat across enrollments, so rows are not independent people.

The exported course-length field is module_presentation_length; the accompanying older codebook calls it length. The imported header is authoritative for the pipeline. The codebook also contains inconsistent enrollment totals; the actual verified table counts above are used in this project.

Missingness and repeated activity

Missing assessment rows and rows with missing marks are different. In the eligible cohort, 3,393 enrollment records have no selected target submission record and 29 have a future target record without a mark. Neither is assigned a zero score.

The activity table is not unique at the learner/resource/day level. Within the eligible 14-day window, 684,147 source rows include 51,964 exact full-row repetitions beyond their first occurrence. Daily keys also occur with differing counts. Their origin is not established by the codebook. The primary count sums all official records; exact-row collapse is a planned robustness encoding. Global full-file uniqueness has not been certified; global duplicate counts within the defined eligible window have been computed.

Interpretation limits

Activity records provide no dwell time, within-day action sequence, question correctness, or displayed-dashboard exposure. Marks have no recorded grade-release timestamp. Earlier marks support retrospective adjustment, but their availability to a live dashboard at the cutoff is unknown. Marks indicate assessed performance; they do not directly measure learning gain or transfer. Offline study is unobserved.
