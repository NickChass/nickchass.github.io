# Nicholai Chasseau — UX Research Portfolio

Personal portfolio site for my UX research case studies.

**Live site:** https://nickchass.github.io

## What's here
- Mixed-methods and quantitative research case studies
- A pre-registered, research-through-design study (OSF)
- Original discovery research carried all the way through to a shipped product
- Built with HTML/CSS — no frameworks

## Case studies
- **Fear of Crime & Psychological Vulnerability** — Master's thesis, 538 participants, regression & mediation analysis (SPSS, Hayes' PROCESS), Trinidad & Tobago
- **Driftline — Screen Time & Wellbeing** — Self-initiated, pre-registered (OSF) experience-sampling study testing whether *how* people appraise screen time predicts wellbeing better than *how much* they use it; working prototype and a validated R (lme4) analysis pipeline
- **AlerTT — Crime Awareness App** — Original UX discovery research (83 participants, Trinidad & Tobago) built directly into a live, working safety app. Study → recommendations → shipped product. **Live app:** https://alertt-tt.netlify.app/
